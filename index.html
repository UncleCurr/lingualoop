How hybrid inference works here

On-device · grammar

Each message gets a quick, single-turn grammar check on your device with Gemini Nano. No audio or text leaves your device for this step.

Cloud · conversation

The conversation itself is multi-turn, so it runs in the cloud with Gemini — the larger model keeps the roleplay natural and remembers the context.

One SDK, both jobs

Firebase AI Logic routes each request to the right place with a single SDK, and tells you where every answer came from.
